# Warranty and Repair Policy

## Coverage

All NovaTech-brand products are covered by a 1-year limited warranty from the date of delivery.
NovaBook laptops are covered for 2 years. The warranty covers defects in materials and
workmanship under normal use, including batteries that fall below 80% capacity within the
warranty period.

## What is not covered

- Accidental damage such as drops, cracked screens or bent frames
- Liquid damage beyond the product's stated water-resistance rating
- Normal wear and tear, including scratches and worn ear cushions
- Damage caused by unauthorized repairs or modifications
- Accessories bought from third parties

## How to make a warranty claim

1. Book an in-store repair appointment (the assistant can do this for you) or visit any
   NovaTech store.
2. Bring the product and your order number. A proof of purchase is attached to your account,
   so a paper receipt is not required.
3. A technician runs diagnostics, usually within 30 minutes.

## Repair turnaround

Most in-warranty repairs are completed within 5-7 business days. If a repair takes longer than
10 business days, we offer a free loaner device for laptops and tablets. If a product cannot be
repaired, we replace it with the same or an equivalent model.

## Out-of-warranty repairs

Diagnostics are free. Typical out-of-warranty prices: headphone battery replacement $49,
watch screen replacement $89, laptop screen replacement $249. You approve the quote before
any work starts.

## Before you hand in a device

Back up your data. Repairs may require a factory reset, and NovaTech is not responsible for
data loss during service.
