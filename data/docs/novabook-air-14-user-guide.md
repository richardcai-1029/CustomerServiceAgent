# NovaBook Air 14 User Guide

## Overview

The NovaBook Air 14 is a lightweight 14-inch laptop with a 2.8K 120 Hz display, the Nova N3
processor, 16 GB of memory and a 512 GB SSD. It weighs 1.25 kg and runs Windows 11 Home.

## Ports and connectivity

- 2 x USB-C (USB4, 40 Gbps, charging and DisplayPort output)
- 1 x USB-A 3.2
- 1 x HDMI 2.1 (supports one 4K display at 120 Hz)
- 3.5 mm headphone/microphone combo jack
- Wi-Fi 7 and Bluetooth 5.4

You can connect up to two external displays: one via HDMI and one via either USB-C port.

## Battery and charging

The battery lasts up to 18 hours of local video playback or about 12 hours of web browsing.
The included 65 W USB-C charger charges the battery to 50% in 30 minutes. Any USB-C Power
Delivery charger rated 45 W or higher works; lower-wattage chargers charge very slowly or only
maintain the charge while the laptop is in use.

To extend battery lifespan, turn on **Battery Care** in the NovaTech Control Center, which
limits charging to 80%.

## Keyboard shortcuts

- **Fn + F4**: toggle the keyboard backlight (3 levels)
- **Fn + F7**: switch performance mode (Quiet / Balanced / Performance)
- **Fn + F10**: turn the webcam privacy shutter on or off
- **Fn + Esc**: lock the function keys

## Troubleshooting

### The laptop won't turn on

Connect the charger and wait 10 minutes, then press and hold the power button for 15 seconds to
force a restart. If the charging LED does not light up, try another USB-C port or charger.

### The laptop is slow or the fan is loud

Switch to Balanced mode with Fn + F7, check for Windows updates, and make sure the air vents on
the bottom are not blocked by soft surfaces such as a bed or sofa.

### Restoring factory settings

Back up your files first. Restart the laptop and press **F11** repeatedly while the NovaTech
logo appears to open NovaTech Recovery, then choose *Reset to factory image*. The process takes
about 40 minutes and erases all data.

## Warranty

The NovaBook Air 14 includes a 2-year limited warranty covering manufacturing defects. Battery
capacity below 80% within the warranty period is covered. Accidental and liquid damage are not.
