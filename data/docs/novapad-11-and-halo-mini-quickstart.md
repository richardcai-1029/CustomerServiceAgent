# NovaPad 11 and Halo Mini Quick Start

## NovaPad 11 tablet

### Specifications

11-inch 2.4K LCD at 90 Hz, 128 GB storage (expandable with microSD up to 1 TB), 8 GB memory,
quad speakers, USB-C 2.0, 8,000 mAh battery with up to 12 hours of video playback. 33 W fast
charging fills the battery in about 2 hours.

### Nova Pen stylus

The Nova Pen (sold separately) attaches magnetically to the right edge of the NovaPad 11, which
pairs and charges it automatically. The pen supports 4,096 pressure levels and palm rejection.
If the pen stops writing, detach it, wait 5 seconds and attach it again to re-pair.

### Kids mode

Open Settings > Family > Kids Mode to create a restricted profile with app limits and screen-time
schedules. A 4-digit PIN is required to leave Kids Mode.

## Halo Mini Smart Speaker

### Setup

1. Plug the speaker into power; the light ring pulses orange.
2. Open the NovaTech Home app, tap **Add device** and choose Halo Mini.
3. Connect to Wi-Fi. The Halo Mini supports both 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz networks, but not
   enterprise (WPA2-Enterprise) networks such as some university or office Wi-Fi.

### Stereo pairs

Two Halo Mini speakers can be grouped as a stereo pair in the NovaTech Home app under
Device > Speaker group.

### Resetting the Halo Mini

Press and hold the mute button for 15 seconds until the light ring turns orange, then set the
speaker up again. Resetting removes it from your NovaTech Home account.

### Troubleshooting

If the Halo Mini does not respond to voice commands, check that the microphone is not muted
(red light ring). If it keeps disconnecting from Wi-Fi, move it closer to the router and avoid
placing it next to a microwave oven.
