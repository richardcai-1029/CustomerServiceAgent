NovaTech Store - Shipping and Returns Guide

SHIPPING OPTIONS
Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days and is free on orders over $50 (otherwise $5.99).
Express shipping takes 1-2 business days and costs $14.99. Orders placed before 2 PM Pacific
Time on a business day ship the same day. We ship within the United States only. All parcels
are delivered by SwiftShip and require no signature unless the order total exceeds $1,000.

CHANGING AN ORDER
You can cancel an order or change its shipping address any time before it ships. Once an order
has shipped, it can no longer be cancelled; you can refuse the delivery or return it after it
arrives instead.

MISSING OR LATE PACKAGES
If tracking shows "Delivered" but you cannot find the package, check with neighbours and your
building's mail room, then contact us within 48 hours. If a package shows no tracking update
for 5 business days, contact us and we will open an investigation with SwiftShip and, if the
package is lost, send a replacement or a full refund.

RETURN CONDITIONS
Most items can be returned within 7 days of delivery. Items must be complete, with all
accessories, manuals and packaging. Opened in-ear headphone ear tips, software licences and
gift cards cannot be returned. Items damaged by the customer are not eligible for a refund.

HOW TO RETURN AN ITEM
1. Ask the assistant to start a return, choose the order and the item(s), and give a reason.
2. A prepaid SwiftShip return label is emailed to you. Pack the item securely and drop it off
   at any SwiftShip location within 7 days.
3. We inspect returns within 1-2 business days of receiving them.

REFUNDS
Refunds go to the original payment method (3-5 business days for cards) or, if you prefer, to
your NovaTech account balance, which is instant once the return is approved. Original shipping
fees are refunded only if the item was defective, damaged or incorrect.

EXCHANGES
We do not process direct exchanges. Return the original item and place a new order; if the new
item is the same model and the original was defective, we waive any express shipping fee.
